🛒 E-commerce Analytics Dashboard

🛍️ E-Commerce Pulse: Sales, Customers & Performance Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze e-commerce sales performance, customer behavior, and product trends—enabling data-driven business decisions across marketing, operations, and revenue strategy.

Description
The E-commerce Analytics Dashboard is a comprehensive and visually intuitive Power BI report built to monitor and evaluate key business metrics such as sales, revenue, orders, customers, and product performance. It helps stakeholders gain actionable insights into business growth, customer purchasing patterns, and overall operational efficiency.

Tech Stack
The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

📊 Power BI Desktop – Primary platform for building interactive dashboards and visual reports

📂 Power Query – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preprocessing

🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Implemented for calculated measures, KPIs, time-based analysis, and dynamic insights

🧩 Data Modeling – Relationships created across sales, customers, products, and date tables to enable cross-filtering

📁 File Format – .pbit (Power BI Template) for reusable and scalable dashboard deployment

Data Source

Source: E-commerce transactional dataset (structured business data)

The dataset contains detailed e-commerce information including:

Order and sales transactions

Product categories and pricing

Customer details

Order dates and time dimensions

Revenue, quantity sold, and profit-related metrics

The data is structured into multiple tables to support efficient analysis and business intelligence reporting.

 Highlights
• Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of transactional data daily, but raw data alone does not provide clear insights.
Key business questions such as:

Which products and categories drive the most revenue?

How are sales trends changing over time?

Which customers contribute the most value?

Where are sales underperforming?

are difficult to answer quickly without a centralized analytical dashboard.

• Goal of the Dashboard

The primary goal of this dashboard is to:

Provide a single source of truth for e-commerce performance

Enable quick monitoring of KPIs like sales, orders, and customers

Support data-driven decisions for marketing, inventory, and growth strategy

Identify trends, patterns, and opportunities across products and customers

• Walkthrough of Key Visuals

Key KPIs (Top Section)

Total Sales / Revenue

Total Orders

Total Customers

Total Quantity Sold

Average Order Value

These KPIs provide an instant snapshot of overall business performance.

Time-Based Sales Analysis (Line / Area Chart)

Visualizes sales trends over time

Helps identify seasonality, growth patterns, and performance fluctuations

Sales by Category / Product (Bar Chart)

Ranks product categories or top-selling products

Highlights revenue drivers and underperforming items

Customer Analysis Visuals

Distribution of customers by purchase value

Identifies high-value and repeat customers

Geographical or Segment-Based Sales (If applicable)

Shows sales performance across regions or segments

Useful for targeted marketing and expansion strategies

Interactive Filters & Slicers

Date slicers

Category / product filters

Customer-based filtering

These allow users to dynamically explore the data and customize insights.

• Business Impact & Insights

Revenue Optimization: Identifies top-performing products and categories to focus sales and marketing efforts

Customer Insights: Helps understand customer behavior and purchasing patterns

Operational Efficiency: Assists in inventory planning and demand forecasting

Strategic Decision-Making: Enables leadership to make informed decisions using real-time insights

Scalability: The template-based design allows reuse with new datasets easily
